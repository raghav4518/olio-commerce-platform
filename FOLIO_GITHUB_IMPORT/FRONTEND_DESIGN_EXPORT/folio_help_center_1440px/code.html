Popular Questions

All FAQs
Why is an ebook available in Web Reader but not downloadable as EPUB? expand_more

Download permissions are set by each edition’s publisher or rights holder. Titles restricted to Web Reader can be read across your desktop and mobile browsers with synced progress.

My UPI payment is pending — when will my book unlock? expand_more

Most UPI settlements clear within 60 seconds. If your bank takes longer, the order appears as Verification Pending under My Orders and unlocks automatically once confirmed.

How do I download a tax invoice for my order? expand_more

Open My Orders, select the order you need, and choose Download Invoice from the Order Details header.
