To design a quiet hour is not to retreat from responsibility; it is to refuse the habit of reacting before an observation has matured. In the studio as in the library, the finest work begins in an unhurried margin.

When we read slowly, we are not merely decoding words—we are testing our own assumptions against another mind’s patience.

Notice how differently a chapter feels when you close the adjacent tabs, set a comfortable measure across the page, and allow your eye to move at the pace of speech rather than the pace of a feed.
